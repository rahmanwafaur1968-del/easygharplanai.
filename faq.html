What is EasyGharPlan AI?

What details do I need to provide?

To prepare your plan, we require:

  • Plot Size & Dimensions
  • Plot Facing Direction
  • Number of Bedrooms
  • Number of Floors
  • Parking Requirement (Optional)
  • Special Requirements (Optional)
05

How long does it take to receive my plan?

Most plans are delivered within 24 to 48 hours after receiving complete information and payment confirmation.

06

Can I request changes to the plan?

Yes. Minor modifications based on your original requirements can be requested after reviewing the initial concept plan.

07

Is the plan suitable for construction?

The plans provided are conceptual plans intended for preliminary planning and visualization.

Before construction, you must obtain detailed architectural drawings, structural design, municipal approvals, and any other approvals required by local authorities.
09

Can you design plans for any plot size?

Yes. We can prepare concept plans for:

  • Small residential plots
  • Medium-sized plots
  • Large residential plots
  • Corner plots
  • Irregular-shaped plots (subject to information provided)
10

Will I receive a CAD or AutoCAD file?

No. The standard packages include PDF delivery only.

If CAD files or additional services are offered in the future, they will be listed separately.
11

Do you provide structural drawings?

Yes. Structural drawings must be prepared by a qualified structural engineer.

12

Is the construction cost estimate included?

At present, our primary service is home planning. Construction cost estimates may be provided as indicative guidance based on project requirements and location, but actual costs can vary depending on materials, specifications, labor rates, and market conditions.

13

Can I use the plan for municipal approval?

No. The plans provided by EasyGharPlan AI are not municipal approval drawings and should not be submitted directly for approvals.

14

What if my plot is located outside India?

We can provide conceptual plans for plots located in many countries. However, local building regulations and approval requirements must always be verified by local licensed professionals.

15

Why are your prices so affordable?

Our goal is to make preliminary home planning accessible to everyone. By using AI-assisted workflows and practical engineering expertise, we can provide affordable concept plans without the high costs typically associated with detailed architectural services.
